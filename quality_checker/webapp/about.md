# About scenario.quality.checker

scenario.quality.checker is an open-source quality gate for OpenSCENARIO scenario files. It
validates a scenario against the ASAM OpenSCENARIO schema, looks for structural problems in
the scenario description, and checks whether the described motion stays physically
plausible. The findings, the dynamics plots and the PDF and CSV reports are meant to be read
before a scenario is used for simulation or as a test case.

## Contributors welcome!

scenario.quality.checker is open source, and contributions are more than welcome. You can
find the source code, report issues, and contribute via
[https://github.com/ika-rwth-aachen/scenario_quality_checker](https://github.com/ika-rwth-aachen/scenario_quality_checker).

The project is maintained by the [Institute for Automotive Engineering (ika) at RWTH Aachen
University](https://www.ika.rwth-aachen.de/) and released under the MIT license. Questions
are welcome at opensource@ika.rwth-aachen.de.
